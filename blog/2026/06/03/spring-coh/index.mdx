The 2026 Spring Court of Honor was held on June 3rd for both Troops 331 and 303.

{/* truncate */}

Many honors were handed out, including a number of rank-ups and several merit
badges! Lots of parents were called up to be pinned by their Scout!

In addition to the awards, Troop 331 provided entertainment in the form of songs
and skits. Afterwards, a delicious array of snacks were quickly gobbled up by
those in attendance.

Congrats to all of the Scouts who earned awards! Be sure to look through the
image gallery to see all the highlights from the evening.
