Troop 331 is Hendricks County’s original all girl troop. Recently the troop had the opportunity to tour the Indianapolis NOAA office. The troop was able to tour the command center and speak with the meteorologist that cover all of central Indiana. They learned about multiple weather models and how they are used to predict and warn on potentially dangerous weather.

{/* truncate */}

They learned in detail all the responsibilities that the office has for this area and surrounding areas.  The girls got to view and learn about all the different tools from Doppler to basic weather reading devices used in this role. Lastly they learned of the education requirements for anyone interested in becoming a meteorologist either on TV or not on TV.

In June of this year Troop 331 was at Summer Camp in Bloomington, IN when a severe storm came through and the troop had to take inclement weather precautions. That storm ultimately produced a tornado to the north of us. The meteorologist were able to pull up the data from that storm the girls experienced and walked them through exactly how it developed and how the weather station assisted in everyone safety that night.
