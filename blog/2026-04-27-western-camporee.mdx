The Scouts of the Legendary Troop 303 attended the 2026 Western Division Spring
Camporee this past weekend. With a theme of Explore the Skies, the Scouts were
able to work on the Astronomy and Weather merit badges.

On Saturday night, at the Order of the Arrow callout out ceremony, Dylan D, Jack S, and Myles
S were all called out. Order of the Arrow is Scouting's nationwide honor society
and represents that these Scouts are looked up to by their fellow troop mates.
They will have the opportunity to go through Ordeal and become members of the
Order of the Arrow. Congrats to these Scouts!

Overall, a great weekend was had by all who attended and the troop looks forward
to future adventures!
