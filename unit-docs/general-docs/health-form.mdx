## General Information

A requirement of Scouting America is to have an active Health and Medical Form
on file for each Scout, leader, and any other adult attending outings. Outings
are defined as anything that is not a normal scout meeting. If a medical form is
not on file, the individual may not attend.

Health forms are provided to healthcare professionals during emergencies if
necessary.

## Expiration

The Health and Medical Forms remain valid for one year from the date of
signature. For general information, please refer to
[Scouting America: Annual Health and Medical Record](https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/ahmr/).
Medical forms from previous years will be securely destroyed.

## Paper Versus Digital

According to Scouting America policy, units and leaders are not authorized to
routinely obtain or retain digital copies of members' medical forms. Digital
copies may only be uploaded when required by a camp or high adventure base, and
exclusively for the duration necessary to complete that process.

Paper copies should be submitted directly to leaders during meetings or at
drop-off for outings. **Medical forms should not be emailed** unless
specifically requested in relation to a particular event.

It is recommended to maintain a digital version on your own device of your
medical forms for ease of updating in future years. Additionally, having extra
copies may be beneficial for activities and events such as OA, Firecrafter, or
outings outside our unit. Please note that summer camp may not always return
submitted medical forms; therefore, those attending camp should ensure they
provide two copies of medical forms AB.

## Medical Forms

This document contains links to the various medical forms. Please download the
correct form and provide us with printed copies and a copy of medical insurance
cards if you have insurance.

All members and participants must submit **Form A and B along with a copy of
their insurance card**. If you are attending Summer Camp or High Adventure
trips, you also need to provide **Form C**, which must be signed by a licensed
practitioner.

All forms can be found here:
[Scouting America: Annual Health and Medical Record](https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/ahmr/)

## Forms A and B

For Scouts or adults who are only going to outings less than 72 hours (i.e.,
weekend camp outs) you will need forms A and B.

[Form AB](https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/HealthSafety/pdf/680-001_AB.pdf)

- Provide us with a copy of Forms A and B with a copy of insurance cards.

## Form C and Summer Camp / High Adventure Details

For Scouts or adults going to summer camp for any period of time aside from the
family night visit and/or high adventure trips; you need form A, B, and C and a
copy of the insurance cards. Please keep an extra copy of form C at home.

[Form ABC](https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/HealthSafety/pdf/680-001_ABC.pdf)

- Form C requires a physical examination conducted by a licensed practitioner,
  with their signature provided. It is advisable to scan and retain a copy of
  this document for your records so that it can be reprinted if necessary.
- Without form C, you or your Scout cannot enter camp and must be picked up
  immediately.
- Please be advised that, due to local or state regulations, we may not receive
  medical forms back from summer camp.
- All medications that will be brought to camp must be clearly listed on the
  required forms.
- It would be the best practice to provide us with the following:
  - A copy of Forms A and B with a copy of insurance cards.
  - A copy of Forms A, B, and C with a copy of insurance cards for camp.
    - This copy will either be retained by the camp due to state law or returned
      to you after camp.
- Check that EpiPens or asthma inhalers are in date and their device date
  matches the date listed on form B. Camp medical staff will verify this.

## Filling in the Forms

Please ensure that all data are filled out as thoroughly and accurately as
possible. In the event that you or your child becomes unconscious or injured,
this information will be provided to first responders and hospital personnel.

All signatures must be handwritten in ink; electronic signatures are not
recommended.

#### Non-prescription Medication

To allow your youth to receive non-prescribed medication, sign below the
medication section on page 2 of Form B. You may note exceptions. Without a
signature, we cannot give over-the-counter medication like Tylenol, ibuprofen,
or antibiotic cream.

![Form B - Non-prescription
Medications](/img/unit-docs/general-info/health-form/form-b-non-prescription-medication.webp)

#### Immunizations

For all participants, Tetanus, Pertussis, MMR, Polio, and Chicken Pox are
required on the form. These will be specifically checked during Summer Camp or
High Adventure check-in. Please fill out your child's immunization information
as accurately as possible. Many camps no longer accept "Up to Date" or a
crossed-out line in the date fields.

![Form B - Immunizations](/img/unit-docs/general-info/health-form/form-b-immunization.webp)

If you or your doctor use MyChart, you can find this information there. All
other immunizations are optional to include.

Immunization records are also available from the State of Indiana via
[MyVaxIndiana](https://eportal.isdh.in.gov/MyVaxIndiana/Default2.aspx)

If you or your child is not immunized, a waiver must be attached to the health
form and can be found here:
[Waiver Form](https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/pdf/680-451.pdf).

### Form C

Form C must be signed and dated. Before leaving the doctor's office, check that
it is complete. Otherwise, you may need to pick up your scout or submit an
updated form. Most camps do not accept emailed copies and leaders at camp cannot
print forms.

![Form C - Signature](/img/unit-docs/general-info/health-form/form-c-signature.webp)
