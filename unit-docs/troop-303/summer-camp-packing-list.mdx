## Before You Pack

- [ ] Label all gear, clothing, water bottles, and equipment with: **Scout Name
  / Troop 303**
- [ ] Check the weather forecast
- [ ] Pack clothing is zip-top bags to stay organized and dry
- [ ] Let your Scout help pack so they know where everything is
- [ ] Wear Class A Uniform, neckerchief, and woggle to camp

## Important Camp Rules

- <i class="fa-solid fa-xmark" style={{color:"red"}} /> No electronics
- <i class="fa-solid fa-xmark" style={{color:"red"}} /> No food in tents or backpacks
- <i class="fa-solid fa-xmark" style={{color:"red"}} /> Do not bring matches or fire starters
- [ ] Bring Scout Handbook (in a zip-top bag)
- [ ] Bring Totin' Chip if carrying a pocketknife
- [ ] Bring Firem'n Chit if earned
