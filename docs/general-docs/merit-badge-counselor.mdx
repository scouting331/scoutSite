## Unlisted Merit Badges

If a Scout is interested in earning a particular merit badge not listed above,
several alternative resources are available:

- **Scoutbook Search**: Scoutbook maintains a comprehensive, searchable database
  of active merit badge counselors.
- **District & Council Events**: Seasonal events like summer camps, merit badge
  midways, and Scouting University offer excellent opportunities to complete
  badge requirements.
