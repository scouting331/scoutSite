---
title: Warm/Hot Weather Packing List
description: A suggested packing list for warmer weather
---

Recommend packing in storage tote, backpack or gym bag and put each day's
set of clothes in a Ziploc bag.

Clothing and personal property should be permanently marked with Scout's
name and Troop number.

:::warning

**Medications must be in original containers** with Scout's name,
medication name and dose and in a plastic bag and then given to Leader
prior to leaving for camp.

:::

## Clothing (adjust for weather forecast)

- [ ] Official Scouting America Uniform
  - Wear to camp and other times during camp
- [ ] Socks - 2 pair for each day, at least
- [ ] T-shirts (Class "B" and/or non-offensive)
- [ ] Underwear (for each day + extra)
- [ ] Long Pants (at least 1 pair)
- [ ] Shorts
- [ ] Sleepwear
- [ ] Hiking Shoes/Boots (broken in)
- [ ] Extra Pair of Shoes
- [ ] Raingear/Poncho
- [ ] Light Jacket/Sweatshirt
- [ ] Water shoes as applicable
- [ ] Bandana—sweat control
- [ ] Bathing suit as applicable and beach towel
  - activity appropriate; no bikinis

## Toiletries/Hygiene

Showers may not be available on short term camps

- [ ] Lip balm
- [ ] Toothbrush and Toothpaste
- [ ] Comb or Brush
- [ ] Hair ties
- [ ] **Deodorant**
- [ ] Shampoo
- [ ] Soap in a carrier or plastic bag
- [ ] Towels and Washcloths
- [ ] Shower Shoes/cheap plastic sandals

## Camping Gear

- [ ] Flashlight/lantern (with extra batteries)
- [ ] REUSABLE Water Bottle or Canteen
- [ ] Multiple plastic shopping bags and/or trash bags for wet/dirty items

## Bedding

- [ ] Sleeping Pad
- [ ] Sleeping Bag or Blankets (multiple if colder; check temp rating on sleeping bag)
- [ ] Flat Twin Sheet – helps on warm nights
- [ ] Pillow and Pillowcase
- [ ] Tent or Mosquito Netting

## Other Items (as applicable)

- [ ] Sunscreen
- [ ] Bug Spray
- [ ] Personal First Aid Kit
- [ ] Medications (see note above)
- [ ] Scouts BSA Handbook—in ziploc
- [ ] MB Pamphlets/Work Sheets
- [ ] Bible/Prayer Book
- [ ] Watch
- [ ] Hat/Cap
- [ ] Sunglasses
- [ ] Pocket Knife - only if they have Totin' Chip
- [ ] Notebook and Pencils for Assignments
- [ ] Compass
- [ ] Camera and Film – disposable is good
- [ ] Money (not usually needed but may have a Trading Post with souvenirs and snacks and MB extra fees)
- [ ] Kleenex
- [ ] Day pack/string bag
- [ ] Camp Chair

- <i class="fa-solid fa-xmark" style={{color:"red"}} /> NO Axes, saws, fireworks, other weapons
