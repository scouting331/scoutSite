## Weekly Meeting Details

Our weekly meetings for both the troops and the pack provide a space for scouts
to work on advancement, plan upcoming events, and build lasting friendships—all
under the dedicated guidance of our volunteer leaders.

<MeetingLocations />

:::tip[Parent Pro Tip]

Meetings strictly follow the school calendar. If Brownsburg Community School
Corporation classes are canceled or on holiday break, we do not meet!
