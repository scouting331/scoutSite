# Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and take care to only collect information that is necessary to keep this site running smoothly.

## What data we collect

- We do not collect personal information from visitors browsing the public website.
- If you click external links, those websites may collect data independently.
- Embedded content such as the Google Calendar and YouTube video are loaded through the services' own platforms and may use their own cookies.

## Embedded content

- The recruitment video uses YouTube's `youtube-nocookie.com` domain to minimize tracking.
- The calendar is embedded from Google Calendar and may load resources from Google.

## Contact

If you have questions about privacy or data usage, please contact us at [scoutingunits331@gmail.com](mailto:scoutingunits331@gmail.com).
