# Cookie Policy

This site uses very limited cookies and embedded content to improve the visitor experience.

## How cookies are used

- We do not set marketing cookies directly on this site.
- The site may load third-party cookies from embedded content providers such as Google Calendar and YouTube.
- We use `youtube-nocookie.com` whenever possible for embedded video content.

## Third-party cookies

If you interact with third-party embeds, those services may set their own cookies. We do not control their tracking practices.

## Your choices

- You can choose to block cookies in your browser settings.
- If you prefer not to load embedded content, avoid clicking on third-party videos or calendar widgets.

Questions about cookies? Email us at [webmaster@brownsburgscouts.org](mailto:webmaster@brownsburgscouts.org).
